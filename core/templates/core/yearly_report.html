1. Executive Summary

2026 was our first year using the C-SHAW Hub. We completely stopped using paper registers across all four campuses (APB, APK, DFC, and SWC) and moved our volunteer tracking online.

Why we built it: In past years, volunteers signed paper sheets at events. Counting hours by hand took days, handwriting was hard to read, and mistakes caused disagreements. Important event notices in WhatsApp groups were also easy to miss. The Hub solved this by calculating hours automatically from check-in timestamps and sending email alerts for new events.

The Old Way (Paper Sheets)

  • Executives spent days adding up hours with calculators.
  • Paper forms were easily misplaced, lost, or damaged.
  • Event notices were buried in busy WhatsApp group chats.

The New Way (C-SHAW Hub)

  • Hours calculate automatically when volunteers sign in and out.
  • Students get direct email alerts when an event is posted.
  • Coordinators can view live attendance numbers anytime.

3. What Worked Well (Key Advantages)

The main features that saved time and made the programme easier to manage this year:

Time Saving

Easy Hour Tracking

Sign-in and sign-out timestamps calculate volunteer hours automatically. This saved coordinators and executives a lot of time by removing manual math and paperwork.

Events

Camp & Hike Excursions

Ticketing for the Year-End Camp and the Hike was handled online. We collected T-shirt sizes easily and generated digital passes that made volunteers feel valued and special.

Learning

Online Learning Hub (LMS)

Volunteers completed health training modules and quizzes from home at their own pace, earning points and completion certificates without travel costs.

Careers

Career Toolkit

Students generated CV summaries, LinkedIn sections, and downloaded an official signed PDF report of their verified hours and awards to use for job applications.

Communication

Simple Event Management

Posting an event with spot limits and sending direct email alerts kept attendance organized and stopped important info from getting lost in WhatsApp.

Fairness

Fair Awards with Clear Metrics

Year-end awards were calculated using clear numbers (hours, events attended, punctuality, and quiz scores) rather than guessing or personal favorites.

Privacy

Safe System & POPIA Rules

Student records and personal details were kept secure with zero data loss, zero security incidents, and full compliance with POPIA rules.

Student Help

Bug Bounty Rewards

We launched a small bounty program where peer educators earned rewards for reporting bugs and suggestions, helping us fix issues quickly while encouraging tech interest.

4. Challenges We Faced

Being honest about what did not work well is important for improving the system next year:

Hours Verification & Executive Trust

Because sign-ins and manual hours were logged by executives on site, some volunteers worried about honesty, potential friend favours, or unfair hour allocations.

Fix: Add dual sign-offs by two executives and clear audit logs on all manual hours.

Setup & Logistics Hours

Volunteers who arrived hours early to pack and set up gazebos received the same flat hours as peers who only attended the event.

Fix: Give extra hours or multipliers for physical setup and packing work.

5. Future Improvements 2027

Planned updates based on student feedback and this year's experience:

1. Better Auditing

Require two executives to approve manual hours and keep an audit history to avoid any dishonesty or suspicion of favoritism.

2. Mobile App

Build a simple mobile app (Android & iOS) for push notifications and offline check-in when campus Wi-Fi drops.

3. Condom & Stock Count Tracking

Add a stock count feature so volunteers can log how many condoms, test kits, and pamphlets they distribute during campaigns.

4. Campus-Wide Student Features

Add tools that help the wider student body outside the volunteer team.

5. Motivation & Retention

Add milestone badges and challenges throughout the year so volunteers stay motivated and don't drop out mid-year.

6. Student-First Design

Keep improving the user interface and mobile layout based directly on feedback from peer educators.
