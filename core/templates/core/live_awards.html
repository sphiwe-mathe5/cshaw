How the Power Score & Awards Are Calculated

The leaderboard uses an objective, multi-pillar formula (0–100 Power Score) to evaluate dedication, consistency, punctuality, and self-directed learning.

50%

Verified Hours

Measures total verified volunteer service hours and approved bonuses, normalized against the highest hours logged by any volunteer in the current year.

Score = (Hours / Max Hours) × 50
25%

Event Participation

Rewards sustained engagement and attendance across diverse campus activities, normalized against the highest event count.

Score = (Events / Max Events) × 25
15%

Punctuality & Reliability

Calculates the percentage of attended events where the volunteer signed in on time or early (at or before scheduled activity start time).

Score = (On-Time Arrivals / Total Events) × 15
10%

LMS Learning Mastery

Recognizes self-improvement and peer educator training through gamified points earned by scoring 100% on LMS module quizzes.

Score = (LMS Points / Max Points) × 10

🏆 Award Category Eligibility Rules

Seniors Only Peer Educator of the Year: Reserved strictly for Senior (Returning) volunteers. First-time newcomers cannot win or influence this award standing.
Newcomers Only Best Junior Peer Educator: Recognizes outstanding first-year volunteers (Newcomers) who have demonstrated exceptional early commitment and performance.
All Volunteers Best Performing Campus: Determined by the highest average volunteer Power Score across all active peer educators registered to that campus.
